Political Organization in the Vedic Period

Fact
Governance was based on a tribal structure.
There was no permanent army.
The justice system was reformative; no death penalty.
Taxes were not regular but voluntary.
Jana, Vish, Grama and Kula were administrative units.

Fact

Status of Women in the Vedic Period

Fact
Women enjoyed a better status.
Women had the right to participate in Sabha and Samiti.
Women did not have property rights.
Niyoga and widow remarriage were practiced.
Child marriage, Sati and purdah were not practiced.
Women are referred to as Jayedastam in the Rigveda.
Women had the right to choose their husbands through Swayamvara.
Women underwent the Upanayana ceremony.
Kakshavati, Lopamudra, Ghosha and Vishwavara were learned women.
